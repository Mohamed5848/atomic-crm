# AMI Generator Opportunities — Phase 2: Water, Wastewater & Desalination

- **Prepared:** 2026-10-09
- **Method:** public sources, English and Arabic. Scored with the v2 rubric in [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md).
- **Recency:** "≤ 3 months" means dated on or after 2026-07-09.
- **Labels:** **[FACT]** = cited source · **[EST]** = AI estimate · **[UNVERIFIED]** = could not be confirmed.

## Read this first

- **Why this sector matters:** in April 2026 the Irrigation Minister's readiness review of pumping
  stations explicitly covered **backup diesel generators for power outages**
  ([Egypt Today](https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/146159/Egypt-s-Irrigation-minister-reviews-readiness-of-pumping-stations-ahead)).
  That is direct public evidence that diesel standby is part of how the state runs pumping assets.
- **No lead is HOT (85+).** As in Phase 1, no public source names a procurement contact or publishes a
  genset spec. The fastest path to HOT is the **E&M subcontractor** on each awarded plant: it writes
  the equipment list.
- **Sizing:** every kVA figure is an **[EST]**. It assumes standby for the critical pumping/process
  load, which is common practice but varies by owner spec. Confirm against each project's spec.
- No contact names, emails or phones were guessed. The decision maker is given as a target role.

## Biggest findings

1. **Hassan Allam + MetiPro is now the #1 water EPC to target.** They hold two 2026 awards: the
   Masharif drinking-water plant in Alexandria (175,000 m³/day, Sept 2026) and the Ras El-Hekma STP
   (July 2026). One meeting with MetiPro's E&M procurement covers both.
2. **The desalination PPP wave has reached tender.** In a 22 Sep 2026 report, the Finance Ministry
   listed Suez ($1bn) and Port Said ($800mn) as **out to tender**, and Alamein ($170mn) and Ain Sokhna
   ($710mn) as in **final tendering**. Every bidding consortium needs genset pricing for its offer now.
3. **Passavant Energy & Environment** (Drake & Scull) holds the **electromechanical subcontract** for
   the new Tanta WWTP (100,000 m³/day). It is the direct buyer of plant E&M equipment, so it is AMI's
   single most "buyer-shaped" contact in this phase.
4. **The government water companies publish their tenders on their own websites**, e.g. Luxor
   (lcww.com.eg) and Qalyubia (qlbww.com.eg). A weekly check of these pages is a free tender feed.

## Top opportunities: detail

1. **W1–W4, desalination PPP wave (75 each):**
   - **Why it needs power:** RO plants run large high-pressure and intake pumps. Bidders normally
     include standby generation for critical loads (controls, intake, essential pumps, building
     services). **[EST]**
   - **Why now:** Suez and Port Said are out to tender; Alamein and Ain Sokhna are in final tendering
     (22 Sep 2026).
   - **Who to contact:** the EPC arm of each bidding consortium (estimating/tender manager).
   - **Estimate:** 1,500–6,000 kVA per plant.
   - **First action:** get the bidder lists and send each EPC a budget quote within 7 days.
2. **W6 Masharif WTP + W5 Ras El-Hekma STP (70/75):**
   - **Why it needs power:** a 175,000 m³/day drinking-water plant cannot stop its high-lift
     pumping, and the STP needs aeration and pumping.
   - **Why now:** both were awarded July–September 2026 to the same JV.
   - **Who to contact:** MetiPro E&M procurement.
   - **Estimate:** 4,000–8,000 kVA combined.
   - **First action:** one meeting covering both projects.
3. **W7 Tanta WWTP (70):**
   - **Why it needs power:** a 100,000 m³/day plant.
   - **Why now:** Passavant is the named E&M subcontractor, so it is the buyer.
   - **Estimate:** 2,000–3,000 kVA.
   - **First action:** ask Passavant who supplies the gensets.
4. **W8 Abu Rawash 4th extension (70):**
   - **Why it needs power:** +400,000 m³/day at Egypt's flagship plant.
   - **Why now:** award pending.
   - **Estimate:** 4–8 MVA.
   - **First action:** quote to all bidders.
5. **W9 East Cairo lift stations (65):** lift stations are the classic standby-genset application.
   Find the contractor.
6. **W10 MWRI M&E Department (60):** a recurring buyer that explicitly uses backup diesel. Register
   as a supplier.
