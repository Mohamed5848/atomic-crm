# AMI Generator Opportunities — Phase 1 Report

- **Prepared:** 2026-10-09
- **Scope:** all sectors, first pass (data centers, healthcare, water/wastewater, industrial,
  hospitality/mixed-use, infrastructure)
- **Method:** web research in English and Arabic using public sources only, scored with the v2 rubric
  in [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md)
- **Recency bands:** ≤ 3 months means signals dated on or after 2026-07-09

## Read this first

- **No lead is HOT (85+) yet.** No public source names a decision maker or publishes a generator
  spec/BOQ for any project found. One qualification call per lead (to get the procurement contact and
  the electrical spec) is enough to move the strongest ones into HOT.
- **Decision makers:** no individual procurement/MEP contact could be verified from public sources, so
  every "Decision maker" cell below is a **target role** plus the company's official channel. No names,
  emails or phone numbers were guessed.
- **Generator brand / competitor evidence:** none found for any project. Every competitor note below is
  about who controls the electrical scope, not about a confirmed genset supplier.
- Labels used: **[FACT]** = stated in a cited source · **[EST]** = AI estimate with reasoning ·
  **[UNVERIFIED]** = claim made by one party that could not be confirmed.
